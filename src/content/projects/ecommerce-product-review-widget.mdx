## Overview

An embeddable photo and video review widget for e-commerce websites, primarily focused on fashion and clothing retailers.

The widget allows customers to record video reviews or take photos directly through their browser using a webcam or device camera, making it easy to share authentic feedback about purchased products.

Designed for integration into existing online stores, the solution includes a customer-facing review widget and a separate administration panel for managing clients and their integrations.

## Approach

- **Widget Development.** Developed an embeddable review widget using Vue.js and Nuxt.js, designed for integration into product pages of existing e-commerce websites.
- **Camera Integration.** Implemented browser-based camera access using HTML5 Media APIs, allowing customers to capture photos and record video reviews directly from their devices.
- **Video Recording and Preview.** Built video recording, playback, and preview workflows, handling different media states and user interactions.
- **Review Submission.** Developed interfaces for reviewing captured media and submitting photo and video reviews through the widget.
- **E-commerce Integration.** Worked on integrating the widget into third-party online stores, including a fashion retail integration.
- **Administration Panel.** Contributed to a separate administrative interface for adding and managing clients and their widget integrations.
- **Project Leadership.** Coordinated development activities and contributed to technical decisions while working on the widget and administration interfaces.
- **Stripe Payment Integration.** Integrated Stripe to support payment processing, connecting the frontend with payment workflows and handling payment-related interactions.

## Results

Developed a **reusable photo and video review widget** that could be integrated into existing e-commerce product pages.

Implemented **browser-based camera functionality, video recording, media preview, and review submission workflows**, allowing customers to create reviews **without installing additional software**.

Contributed to the **administration panel** used to manage clients and support widget integrations across online stores.

Delivered the **core functionality of the solution**, combining browser media capabilities with reusable Vue.js components and e-commerce integration workflows.
