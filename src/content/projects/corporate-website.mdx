## Overview

The official corporate website of GroupBWT, a software development company, designed to showcase its services, expertise, projects, and company information.

Built on WordPress, the website serves as a key marketing and communication channel, supporting content updates, search engine visibility, and lead generation.

The project involved ongoing development, modernization of legacy functionality, performance optimization, and close collaboration between developers, designers, and the marketing team.

## Approach

- **Technical Leadership.** Led the technical development of the website, coordinated developers, assigned tasks, reviewed merge requests, and maintained code quality standards.
- **Requirements and Planning.** Worked closely with marketing specialists and designers to understand business requirements, translate designs and ideas into technical tasks, and plan their implementation.
- **Developer Onboarding.** Onboarded new developers to the project, explained its architecture and development workflows, and helped them become familiar with the existing codebase.
- **Legacy Code Modernization.** Led refactoring and modernization of existing WordPress functionality, improving code organization, maintainability, and development workflows.
- **Performance Optimization.** Coordinated and contributed to frontend performance improvements, optimizing page loading and resource delivery to achieve **90+ scores in Google PageSpeed Insights**.
- **SEO Optimization.** Worked on technical SEO improvements, including page structure, semantic markup, and frontend optimization to support search engine visibility.
- **Code Quality and Reviews.** Established consistent development practices, reviewed code changes, and helped maintain a reliable and maintainable codebase.

## Results

Led the ongoing **technical development and modernization** of the GroupBWT corporate website **over three years**, coordinating developers and ensuring the implementation of marketing and design requirements.

Improved website loading performance and technical SEO, achieving **90+ scores in Google PageSpeed Insights**.

**Modernized legacy WordPress functionality**, improving code maintainability and simplifying future development.

Established **consistent code review practices** and supported **developer onboarding**, helping new team members become productive and maintain code quality across the project.
